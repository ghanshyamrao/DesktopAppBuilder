About us

Desktop app building, made approachable.

TooDesktop helps makers, founders, and teams turn web products into polished desktop apps without starting from a blank Electron project.

Why TooDesktop exists

Shipping a desktop app should not require weeks of build scripts, installer setup, and platform packaging work. TooDesktop brings those pieces into one visual workflow so you can focus on the product your users already love.

Paste a URL, choose a starter, tune the theme, add desktop actions, and build installers for distribution.
