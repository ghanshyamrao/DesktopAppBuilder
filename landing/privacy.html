Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information TooDesktop ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use our website, builder, and related services (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

a. Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • A password (stored as a salted hash — we never store the plaintext) or, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile information Google shares with us (name, email, profile picture, Google account ID).

b. Project & build data

To build your desktop apps, we may store:

  • Project configurations (app name, target URL, theme settings, window options, icons, branding assets you upload).
  • Build artifacts and logs.

This data exists to operate the Service. You can delete projects at any time from your dashboard.

c. Payment information

If you subscribe to a paid plan, payments are processed by Stripe. We receive limited information from Stripe such as your subscription status, plan, billing country, and the last four digits of your card. We never receive or store your full card number. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to data they collect.

d. Usage & device data

When you use the website or the builder, we automatically collect:

  • IP address (used for security, fraud prevention, and approximate location).
  • Browser or app version, operating system, and screen size.
  • Pages or features you interact with, and timestamps.
  • Error reports and diagnostic logs.

e. Communications

If you email us or contact support, we keep a copy of that correspondence so we can help you.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Build and deliver the desktop apps you create.
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Authenticate you and protect your account.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate about your account or the Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Cookies & similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Authentication — to keep you signed in.
  • Preferences — to remember settings like theme.
  • Security — to detect suspicious activity.
  • Analytics — to understand which features are used (aggregated, no profile building).

You can clear cookies through your browser settings; doing so may sign you out and reset preferences.

5. Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to keep it longer (for example, transaction records for tax and accounting purposes).

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption at rest where appropriate, hashed passwords, access controls, and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to keep yours safe and we will notify you of any incident that materially affects your data, as required by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive a portable copy of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@toodesktop.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. International transfers

TooDesktop may be operated from, and your data may be processed in, countries other than your own. Where required by law, we put in place appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for cross-border transfers.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you by email or in-app notice.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@toodesktop.com.
